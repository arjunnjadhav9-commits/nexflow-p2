Part 1 — Getting Started

Logging In

Nexflow P2 accounts are set up for you when your factory is onboarded — there is no self-signup page. Enter the email and password you were given, and use the eye icon to check what you typed before submitting. If your details are wrong, an error message appears right on the login card.

Understanding Your Role

Every person who logs in has exactly one role: Owner, Supervisor, Storekeeper, Operator, or Accountant. Your role decides which pages appear in your navigation bar and what you're allowed to do — if a page mentioned in this manual doesn't show up for you, your role simply doesn't include it. Part 6 has a full, printable guide for each role.

Language Toggle

Tap the language button in the top navigation bar (मराठी / English) to switch the whole app, including this manual. Your choice is remembered on this device for next time. A few CA/GST-facing screens — CA Export and GSTR-2B Reconciliation — intentionally stay in English, since terms like HSN, GSTIN, and CGST/SGST/IGST are used exactly this way by every CA and on the GST portal itself; translating them would confuse rather than help.

Navigating the App

The top bar shows links for every page your role can access, your initials/email/plan badge on the right, and a Logout button. On a phone, a hamburger icon (☰) opens the same links in a left-side drawer.

Using It on Your Phone

Nexflow P2 is built mobile-first — every form and table works on a phone browser, no app install needed. The QR Scanner (Pro/Founder plans) is designed specifically for phone cameras.

Part 2 — Daily Operations

Dashboard

The Dashboard (index.html) is the first page you land on after login. Four numbers at the top: Total Materials, Low Stock Alerts, OK Stock, and Last Updated. Below that is your full Stock Levels table — Code, Material Name, Current Stock, Min Stock (tap to edit inline), Unit, and a Status badge (⚠ Low Stock / ✓ OK). Tap the 🕒 icon on any row to open its Material History with a From/To date filter across every GRN, consumption, and adjustment. The page refreshes itself every 5 minutes.

GRN — Receiving Materials

On grn.html, pick or add a Supplier, set the Date, then add one row per material received: search by name or code, enter Quantity, Rate, and the supplier's Invoice No, and choose Intrastate (CGST+SGST) or Interstate (IGST) for that row. The GRN Number itself is assigned automatically on submit — you never type it.

⚠
Invoice No is mandatory on every row — submission is blocked without it. The CA Export and GSTR-2B Reconciliation both key off this field to match your purchases to what your supplier filed.

Owners and Supervisors also see a Month-End Check button that opens a GRN Reconciliation view grouped by supplier, with any entry missing an invoice number shown in red — run this before sending your CA Export each month.

Production Issue

On production-issue.html, pick a Product and a Batch Quantity, then tap Generate BOM — Nexflow explodes the recipe into a materials table (each row's quantity = BOM qty per unit × your batch quantity), which you can hand-edit, plus a + Manual Row option for extra ad-hoc materials. Tap Issue Materials to confirm; if any row needs more than what's in stock, Nexflow lists exactly which materials are short and blocks confirmation until it's fixed. Once confirmed, stock is deducted and a challan number is assigned — from there you can print, attach client details to turn it into a dispatch challan, or start a new issue.

Issuing the same product/batch/date twice in one day asks you to confirm you really mean it — a safety net against accidental double entry.

Dispatch — Sending Products

On dispatch.html, fill in Client Information (name autofills from clients you've used before, address, PO number, your own challan reference, dispatch date, transport), add one or more Products with quantities, then Confirm Dispatch. Nexflow checks BOM stock first — if anything's short, it tells you exactly what and how much, and blocks the dispatch. Once confirmed, you get a Consumption Summary showing what was used and any new low-stock warnings, plus a Print Challan option.

RM Dispatch — Sending Raw Materials

rm-dispatch.html uses the same client-information fields as Dispatch, but instead of picking a product with a BOM, you add raw materials directly, one at a time — there's no recipe explosion. Use this when you're sending raw material out (to a client, a subcontractor, anywhere) rather than consuming it to make something. It's the mirror image of GRN: GRN brings material in, RM Dispatch sends it out.

Products & BOM

On products.html, add a product with its Name, an optional Description, Unit (NOS/KG/L/MTR/PCS), and HSN/SAC code. Every product needs a BOM (bill of materials) before it can be issued or dispatched — select the product, then add each raw material and how much of it one unit needs ("if 1 unit needs 2.5 KG of this material, enter 2.5"). Once the BOM is set, issuing or dispatching 10 units automatically deducts 10× that quantity of every ingredient. A green "BOM Set" badge or yellow "No BOM ⚠" badge on the products list tells you which products are ready.

QR Scanner

scanner.html (Pro/Founder plans only) opens your phone's camera to scan a Nexflow-generated QR code on a material — it shows current stock and lets you queue it into a GRN with quantity (and optional rate/invoice number), building a "Pending GRN Items" list you confirm all at once as a single GRN. On a desktop with no camera, it shows a QR code to open the scanner on your phone instead. Lite-plan tenants see a lock notice instead of the scanner.

Invoices

Invoicing isn't its own starting page — you generate one from wherever the dispatch happened. On dispatch.html, rm-dispatch.html, and production-issue.html, a Generate Invoice button appears (Pro/Founder plans only) once a dispatch is confirmed. On all-dispatch-history.html, open a dispatch's Detail modal — it shows "View Invoice →" if one already exists, or a Generate Invoice button if not. Either way you review the rate per item and the GST type (CGST+SGST / IGST / None) before it's created and emailed. Once created, manage and resend every invoice from invoices.html (Invoice No, Date, Client, Mode, Period, Amount, Status, Actions — with a Resend button). The AI Copilot can also generate and send invoices by chat — see Part 3.

Part 3 — AI Copilot

What Is the AI Copilot?

A chat button (bottom-right FAB) that lets you ask questions or record entries in plain Hinglish/Marathi instead of filling forms. It's available only on Pro and Founder plans, and only to Owner and Supervisor roles — Storekeeper, Operator, and Accountant don't get it (see Part 6). Your daily message limit depends on your plan: 30/day on Founder, 50/day on Pro — and that limit is shared per tenant, not per person.

How to Talk to It

Type naturally — mixing Marathi and English in the same sentence works fine ("copper aala 50 kg"). The Copilot falls into one of three behaviours depending on what you ask:

  • Questions get an instant answer — no confirmation needed, nothing is changed.
  • Sending something (a challan, export, or invoice by email) executes immediately, no confirm step — because these are quick, low-risk actions you'd otherwise have found and clicked yourself.
  • Recording something new (a GRN, a production issue, a dispatch) shows you a confirm card first — nothing is written to your data until you say yes.

Tips

💡
Mix Marathi and English freely in the same message — the Copilot understands both together (Hinglish).
💡
You can refer to materials by their material code or by their full name — either works.
💡
The chat widget's quick-tap chips show your top 6 most-used materials for one-tap stock checks — the search box below the chips has your full material list.
💡
If your very first message of the day fails with "Failed to load raw materials," just send it again — this is a known one-time delay on the first request after a period of inactivity, not a bug, and it always works on the second try.

What the Copilot Cannot Do

🚫
Cancel or delete a dispatch, challan, GRN, or invoice — do this from the relevant page (e.g. Dispatch History → Cancel), not via chat.
🚫
Change any Settings — company details, GST numbers, staff, pricing, Telegram setup. All Settings changes must be made directly on settings.html by the owner.
🚫
Edit a GRN's rate or invoice number after the fact — go to grn.html or the GRN history page for that.
🚫
Anything your plan or role doesn't allow — Lite-plan tenants and Storekeeper/Operator/Accountant roles don't get the Copilot at all, regardless of what they type (see Part 6).

Part 4 — Reports & Exports

Stock Dashboard, Monthly Report, CA Report

All three live under the Reports tab. Stock Dashboard is a read-only summary view of current stock across every material. Monthly Report breaks GRN/consumption/dispatch activity down by calendar month. CA Report reconciles opening stock + GRN − consumption = closing stock for a period, valued using each material's latest price from Settings → Prices — this is the figure your CA uses to sanity-check stock movement before filing.

CA Export (Tally / Zoho)

export.html builds the GST-ready spreadsheet your CA imports into Tally or Zoho — 17 columns per purchase line (material, HSN/SAC, GST rate, CGST/SGST or IGST split by purchase_type, Place of Supply, Invoice Total, and more). It's deliberately English-only, same as this section, since these are the exact field names a CA works with.

Updated August 12, 2026 (Phase 1):

  • The GST Summary sheet's title now shows the actual date range you exported ("GST SUMMARY — 01/07/2026 to 31/07/2026"), or "GST SUMMARY — All Time" if you didn't pick a range.
  • "Total ITC Claimable" is now labelled "Input GST Recorded (Potential ITC)" — a more honest label, since it's what you've recorded, not a guarantee the ITC is safe (that's what GSTR-2B Reconciliation below actually checks).
  • The Invoices sheet is now one row per line item (not per invoice) — 19 columns: Invoice No, Invoice Date, Client, Client GSTIN, B2B/B2C, Place of Supply, Invoice Mode, Period From, Period To, Item Description, HSN/SAC, Qty, Unit, Rate, Taxable Amount, CGST (₹), SGST (₹), IGST (₹), Item Total (₹) — with GST split proportionally per item.
  • The Purchases (GRN) sheet gained a Place of Supply column, derived automatically from your supplier's GSTIN state code.

You can download this from export.html, or say "CA la July cha export pathav" to the AI Copilot to have it emailed directly (Part 3).

GSTR-2B Reconciliation

This is the most important reason to be on the Pro or Founder plan. Since July 2026, GST 2.0 enforces hard system-level ITC matching — if a purchase you claim Input Tax Credit on doesn't match what your supplier reported in their GSTR-1, that credit is auto-blocked, and unlike before, it can no longer be fixed manually inside GSTR-3B. That makes catching mismatches before filing a working-capital problem, not a bookkeeping nicety — one blocked claim can tie up ₹50,000+ that would otherwise be yours.

Access: gated in this order — role first, then plan. You must be an Owner or Accountant; anyone else sees:

🚫
"Only the owner or accountant can access this page."

And you must be on Pro or Founder; Lite tenants see:

⚠
"GSTR-2B Reconciliation is available on the Pro and Founder plan. You're currently on Lite. Upgrade to get automatic ITC matching and never miss a blocked credit."

It's reached only through a link on export.html — it isn't in the main navigation bar.

How to use it:

  1. Download your GSTR-2B JSON from the GST portal (available from the 14th of each month): Returns → View GSTR-2B → Download JSON.
  2. Upload it — the period is detected automatically from the file itself.
  3. If the GSTIN in the file doesn't match your Settings GSTIN, you get a non-blocking warning naming both — the reconciliation still runs.
  4. Tap Run Reconciliation. Nexflow groups your GRN rows by supplier GSTIN + normalised invoice number (a multi-material GRN under one supplier bill is summed first) and matches each group against the JSON.

Four buckets you'll see, with a ±₹2 rounding tolerance:

  • ✅ Matched — your GRN and the supplier's GSTR-1 agree. ITC is safe.
  • ⚠️ Amount Mismatch — same invoice, taxable value differs by more than ₹2. Ask the supplier for a credit note or correct your GRN.
  • ❌ ITC Blocked — in your Nexflow GRN but not in the JSON at all. Your supplier hasn't filed their GSTR-1 yet — chase them.
  • ❓ Unrecorded — in the JSON but no matching GRN in Nexflow. Either a purchase you forgot to enter, or something fraudulently auto-accepted. Rows with IMS status NO_ACTION are highlighted red — these were auto-accepted by the GST portal without your review and are the most urgent to check first.

Tap ⬇ Download Reconciliation Report for a 4-sheet XLSX (one sheet per bucket, orange-header styled). Results are session-only — nothing is saved to the database. If you refresh the page, you lose the view and must re-upload and re-run; download the XLSX first if you need to keep a copy.

Monthly Telegram Nudge

On the 15th of every month at 8:00 AM IST, Nexflow sends a Telegram reminder to every tenant with the AI agent enabled and a Telegram chat ID configured, pointing them to run GSTR-2B Reconciliation for the newly-available period. This is separate from the daily 8 AM low-stock digest (see Settings → Telegram Alerts).

History Pages

GRN history, dispatch history (all-dispatch-history.html), production issue history, and RM dispatch history all offer a From/To date filter, Export CSV, and Print. On dispatch/issue history, Cancel reverses the stock movement and cannot be undone — double-check before confirming. GRN history has no cancel or edit option by design; if a GRN was entered wrong, use Stock Adjustment in Settings instead (Part 5).

Part 5 — Settings

settings.html is Owner-only, but every role benefits from knowing what's configurable — so this part is documented for everyone.

Company Details

Company name, address, logo, GSTIN, SAC code, contact email (used as reply-to on challan/invoice emails — fill this in or your clients can't reply to you directly), and bank details (Bank Name / Account No / IFSC, printed on every invoice).

Raw Materials

Add/edit materials with unit, minimum stock level, material code, HSN/SAC, and GST rate. Lite plan is capped at 250 materials — the limit is checked before insert, with an upgrade prompt once you're close. Deleting a material would orphan every BOM that references it, so there's no delete — use Deactivate instead.

Suppliers

Add suppliers with name, mobile, address, and GSTIN (optional, but required for GSTR-2B matching to work at all — a supplier with no GSTIN on file can never be reconciled). Suppliers have an inline Edit row for all four fields. CSV-imported suppliers default to inactive until you review and activate them, so they don't clutter dropdowns before you've checked them.

Clients

Add clients with name, address, PO number, and GSTIN. Email is mandatory if you want to use Send Challan or Send Invoice (by chat or by button) for that client — without it, those actions have nothing to send to.

Products & BOM

Same product/BOM builder described in Part 2, reachable from Settings too — product name, unit, and HSN/SAC code (printed on invoices).

Prices

Two independent price histories: raw material purchase price (used to value your CA Report and GST exports) and finished-product selling price (used to pre-fill invoice rates). Both work the same way — add a new price with an effective date, and the latest one by date is always what's used; you're never editing history, only adding to it.

Challan & GRN Settings

Set your starting challan number, choose Unified (one numbering sequence across all challan types) or Split (separate sequences per dispatch type) numbering mode, and set the default Intrastate/Interstate purchase type new GRN rows start with.

Invoice Starting Number

Set where your invoice numbering (format INV-YYYYMM-NNN) begins.

Staff Management

Pro/Founder plans only. Invite staff by email and assign a role. There's no in-place role change — to correct someone's role, remove them and re-invite with the right one.

Telegram Alerts

Connect your factory's Telegram via BotFather + Chat ID to receive the daily 8 AM digest (low stock, yesterday's GRNs, stalled drafts, no-GRN-in-3-days) and the monthly GSTR-2B nudge (Part 4). Instant alerts also fire automatically after any production issue, dispatch, or RM dispatch that drops a material below its minimum.

Agent Settings & CA Export Email

Turn the AI Copilot on/off, and set the email address the AI Copilot's "send to CA" actions (send_tally_export) deliver to. This tab is hidden entirely for Lite-plan tenants — there's nothing here for them to configure.

Stock Adjustment

The only way to correct a stock number directly, without a GRN or dispatch — used for physical count corrections, damage write-offs, or fixing an old data-entry error. A reason is required for every adjustment, since these bypass the normal GRN/consumption trail and need to be explainable later.

Part 7 — Troubleshooting

Can't Log In

Double-check your email and password (tap the 👁 icon to reveal what you typed). Accounts are created by Nexflow during onboarding — there's no self-signup, so if you've never received login details, contact your Owner or Nexflow support (see Contact Support below), not a signup page.

Scanner Not Working

The camera scanner needs a phone browser with camera access granted, and a Pro or Founder plan — Lite tenants see a lock notice by design, not a bug. On a desktop with no camera, the page shows a QR code to open the scanner on your phone instead — that's expected, not an error.

Stock Showing Wrong Number

Stock is always the sum of every GRN, consumption, and adjustment transaction for that material — it's never stored as a single editable number, so it can't drift out of sync with what actually happened. Tap the 🕒 History icon on the Dashboard for that material to see every transaction and find the one that looks wrong. If a past entry really was wrong, correct it with a Stock Adjustment (Settings) with a clear reason — never by editing history.

Challan Email Not Received

Check Settings → Clients that the client's email address is filled in and correct — Send Challan has nothing to send to without it. Ask the client to check spam/junk. Challan emails have no attachment by design — they contain a link to view and verify the delivery online, so make sure the client actually opened the email rather than just glancing at the subject line.

Invoice Not Generating

Generate Invoice only appears on Pro/Founder plans, and only once a dispatch is confirmed (not draft). It also only appears once per dispatch — if one already exists for that dispatch, you'll see "View Invoice →" instead, since Nexflow blocks a second invoice against the same dispatch. If you're on the Accountant role, this is expected — Accountants can view and print invoices but not generate them (Part 6).

AI Copilot Not Responding

  • "Failed to load raw materials" on your very first message of the day: send it again — known one-time cold-start delay, not a bug.
  • No FAB (chat button) visible at all: your plan is Lite (no Copilot at all), or your role is Storekeeper/Operator/Accountant (Part 6).
  • A message about the daily limit: your tenant's daily quota (30/day Founder, 50/day Pro) is shared across everyone who uses the Copilot, not per person — it resets automatically the next day.
  • It misunderstood you: try rephrasing more directly (name the material/product/challan number explicitly) — see Part 3 for exact phrasing patterns that work well.

GSTR-2B Reconciliation

  • Plan gate: "GSTR-2B Reconciliation is available on the Pro and Founder plan. You're currently on Lite. Upgrade to get automatic ITC matching and never miss a blocked credit."
  • Role gate: "Only the owner or accountant can access this page."
  • Invalid JSON: "This doesn't look like a valid GSTR-2B file. Download it from the GST portal under Returns → View GSTR-2B → Download JSON."
  • GSTIN mismatch between the file and your Settings: a non-blocking warning naming both GSTINs — reconciliation still runs.
  • No GRN data found for that period: a non-blocking notice — reconciliation still runs (it'll just show everything as Unrecorded).
  • Nothing matching: matching keys on supplier GSTIN + invoice number — a supplier with no GSTIN on file (Settings → Suppliers) can never match, no matter how correct the invoice number is.
  • Results disappeared after a refresh: expected — results are session-only by design, not saved anywhere. Download the XLSX report if you need to keep a copy, and re-run when you need to look again.
