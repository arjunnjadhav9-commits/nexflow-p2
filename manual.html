PART 1

Getting Started

Login, Dashboard, and Navigation — what you need before day one.

What is Nexflow P2?

Nexflow P2 tracks raw material stock, production, and client deliveries for your factory — replacing paper registers and challan books with one system that works from your phone or computer. GRN records what comes in from suppliers, Issue records what goes to the production floor, Dispatch records what leaves for a client and generates a challan number, and Reports & Invoices pull it all together for your CA and your billing.

✓
You do not need technical knowledge to use Nexflow P2. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this system.

How to Log In

  1. Open the Nexflow P2 link in Chrome browser (phone or computer).
  2. Enter your Email and Password.
  3. Tap the eye icon to check for typos before submitting.
  4. Tap Sign In. Correct credentials land you on the Stock Dashboard.
!
There is no "Forgot Password" link. Contact your factory Owner or Nexflow support to reset it. New team members must be invited by the Owner from Settings → Staff — nobody can sign up on their own.

PART 2

Daily Operations

GRN, Issue, Dispatch, RM Dispatch, and Invoices — the five workflows your team runs every day.

1. GRN — Receiving Materials

Log every supplier delivery here — this is what increases your stock. Do it the same day, before material gets mixed into general stock.

Manual Entry

  1. Open GRN from the menu.
  2. Select the Supplier from the dropdown (tap "+" to add a new one).
  3. Confirm the Date (defaults to today).
  4. Search for the material — Unit fills in automatically.
  5. Enter Qty and Rate.
  6. Enter Invoice No — mandatory on every row since August 7, 2026.
  7. Choose Intrastate or Interstate for this delivery — this decides whether the GST math on your CA export/Tally/Zoho uses CGST+SGST or IGST.
  8. Tap + Add Row for additional materials from the same delivery.
  9. Tap Submit All Materials — confirmation shows the assigned GRN number.
!
Always check the Unit (KG vs PCS vs MTR) before entering quantity — a wrong unit throws off your entire stock count. GRN entries cannot be edited or deleted once submitted; use Settings → Stock Adjustment for genuine corrections.

Scanner GRN — Mobile, Pro/Founder Plan Only

Scan the QR code sticker on each material's shelf instead of typing. Works only in Chrome on Android phones.

  1. Open Scanner on your phone (Chrome). Allow camera access.
  2. Optionally select Supplier and confirm Date at the top.
  3. Point the camera at the QR sticker on the shelf — the phone vibrates when detected.
  4. Type Quantity, optionally add Rate and Invoice No, tap Add to GRN.
  5. Camera resets immediately — repeat for every material in the delivery.
  6. Tap Confirm GRN when done — all items save under one GRN number.
⚠️
Scanning does NOT save immediately — you must tap Confirm GRN at the end, or your pending list is lost if you close the page. Does not work on iPhone or desktop.

Month-End GRN Reconciliation

A modal on the GRN page (Owner/Supervisor only) that groups every GRN by supplier and shows invoice numbers side by side — a red dash marks a missing invoice number, and an orange "Unknown Supplier" group catches anything that couldn't be matched. Run this before CA Export or GSTR-2B Reconciliation each month so nothing is missing an invoice number when it matters.

2. Issue — Materials to Production

Records raw materials leaving the store for the production floor. The system auto-calculates quantities from the product recipe (BOM) and deducts them from stock.

  1. Open Issue from the menu, select the Product being produced.
  2. Enter the Batch Quantity (number of units, e.g. 36 motors) and confirm the Issue Date.
  3. Tap Generate BOM ↓ — the recipe loads and calculates quantities.
  4. Review the list, adjust Issue Qty if needed, tap Issue Materials.
  5. If any material is short, Confirm is disabled — receive more via GRN first.
  6. Tap Confirm Issue — stock is deducted immediately.
⚠️
Most common mistake: forgetting to tap Generate BOM first — nothing issues until the recipe loads. Wrong Batch Quantity multiplies across every ingredient.

3. Dispatch — Finished Products to Clients

Records finished products going to a client. Raw materials are deducted automatically via BOM, and a challan number is generated. Since August 7, the stock check aggregates required quantity per material across all BOM lines before checking balance — so the same material appearing in multiple recipe lines can no longer slip past a stock check individually and then fail combined.

  1. Open Dispatch, type Client Name — address auto-fills from previous orders.
  2. Fill PO Number, Dispatch Date, Transport (vehicle number).
  3. Select a Product, enter Qty, tap + Add Product — repeat for all products.
  4. Tap Confirm Dispatch — the system checks raw material stock.
  5. If any material is short, dispatch is blocked entirely — you cannot partially dispatch.
  6. Tap Print Challan to open the challan document, or send it by email via AI Copilot: Challan 4325 KPML la pathav

4. RM Dispatch — Sending Raw Materials Out

Use when raw material itself leaves the factory — to a job-worker or sold directly. There is no BOM calculation; you enter exactly what leaves.

  1. Open RM Dispatch, type Client Name (required).
  2. Search and select the Material, check the stock colour before confirming.
  3. Enter Qty, tap + Add — repeat for all materials.
  4. Tap Confirm Dispatch — stock is deducted and a challan is generated.
⚠️
RM Dispatch does NOT block you from dispatching more than your stock — it only shows a warning. Always read the stock colour before confirming (red = no stock).

5. Invoices — Billing Your Clients

Generate billing documents with GST for dispatched goods — Pro and Founder plans only. This is a proforma billing document, not a GST filing return; your CA still handles GSTR filing separately.

Single Invoice

  1. Go to All Dispatches → find the confirmed challan → tap Detail.
  2. Tap Generate Invoice, enter Rate per item, choose GST Type.
  3. Tap Generate Invoice — opens in a new tab with a Download PDF button.

Consolidated Invoice

  1. Open Invoices from the menu → tap + New Consolidated Invoice.
  2. Select Client, Date From, Date To, GST Type, tap Generate Invoice.
  3. Every confirmed delivery to that client in the date range is combined into one invoice.

Send either kind by AI Copilot:

  • KPML la invoice pathav — latest invoice, right now
  • KPML la July cha invoice pathav — consolidated July invoice
!
Invoice amount locks when sent — changing prices later will NOT update it. Only Owner and Supervisor can generate or cancel invoices; Accountant can view and print only.

Tips & Limitations

  • Mixing Marathi and English in one sentence is fine — that's how it's designed.
  • If there are similar material names, add the material code to be specific.
  • Tap material chips above the message box to insert a name without typing.
  • If the very first message of the day fails, send it again — it's a one-time cold-start delay, not an error.

What the Copilot Cannot Do

  • Cancel any challan, dispatch, GRN, or invoice — use History pages instead.
  • Delete data.
  • Change Settings — company details, prices, staff, Telegram must be changed by the Owner.

Reports & Exports

Monthly reports, CA exports, GSTR-2B reconciliation, and history pages for full visibility.

Stock Dashboard

Check it every morning before starting production for Low Stock Alerts. Auto-refreshes every 5 minutes.

Monthly Report & CA Report

ToolWhat you getHow to use
Monthly Report Opening stock, GRN, Consumption, Net Change, Closing Stock per material. Month + Year → Generate Report → Print.
CA Report Formal document for your CA — all GRN, Consumption, Dispatch, GST details for a date range. From + To Date → Generate CA Report → new tab → Print / Save as PDF.

CA Export (Tally / Zoho Format)

An XLSX workbook built for your CA — send it from the Dashboard, export.html, or by AI Copilot (CA la export pathav).

What's inside

  • Sheet 1 — Purchases (GRN): every GRN row with Supplier Name, Supplier GSTIN, Supplier Invoice No, HSN/SAC, GST rate, and a Place of Supply column (mapped from the supplier's GSTIN state code).
  • Sheet 2 — Invoices: one row per line item (not per invoice) — 19 columns including Client GSTIN, B2B/B2C, Place of Supply, HSN/SAC, and CGST/SGST/IGST computed proportionally per item from the invoice's total GST.
  • Sheet 3 — GST Summary: titled with the actual date range you exported (or "All Time"), showing "Input GST Recorded (Potential ITC)", output tax, and net payable totals.

Purchase-type aware: a GRN row marked Interstate uses IGST columns; Intrastate splits CGST+SGST. Consumption rows were removed from this export in August 2026 — your CA only needs purchases and invoices, not internal stock movement.

✓
Add your CA's email under Settings → Agent → CA Export Email first, otherwise the Copilot export command has nowhere to send it.

GSTR-2B Reconciliation

From July 2026, GST 2.0 introduced hard system-level matching — Input Tax Credit that doesn't match your GSTR-2B is auto-blocked, and can no longer be manually corrected in GSTR-3B. This page catches mismatches before that happens, by matching your own GRN purchases against the GSTR-2B file the GST portal generates from what your suppliers have filed.

🔒
Pro and Founder plans only, and only the Owner or Accountant role can open this page — not reachable via the navbar, only from a link on the CA Export page.

How to use it

  1. Download the GSTR-2B JSON for the period from the GST portal (Returns → View GSTR-2B → Download JSON).
  2. Upload it — the period is auto-detected from the file itself, and matching runs entirely in your browser (the file is never sent to any server).
  3. If the GSTIN in the file doesn't match your own, you'll see a non-blocking warning naming both — the reconciliation still runs.
  4. Tap Run Reconciliation — GRN rows are grouped by supplier GSTIN + invoice number (a multi-material GRN batch under one supplier invoice is summed first) and matched against the file.

The four result buckets

BucketMeaning
✅ MatchedSupplier GSTIN + invoice number found in the file, taxable value within ₹2.
⚠️ Amount MismatchKeys match, but the taxable value differs by more than ₹2.
❌ ITC BlockedIn your GRN, but not in the GSTR-2B file — your supplier hasn't filed GSTR-1 for it yet.
❓ UnrecordedIn the GSTR-2B file, but no matching GRN in Nexflow — an unrecorded purchase, or a fraudulent IMS auto-accept. Rows with IMS status NO_ACTION are highlighted red as most urgent to review.

A "Download Reconciliation Report" button exports an XLSX with 4 sheets — Matched, Amount Mismatch, ITC Blocked, Unrecorded — with orange headers matching the rest of the app.

⚠️
Results are session-only — nothing is saved to the database, so they disappear if you refresh the page. Download the XLSX if you need to keep a copy, and re-run reconciliation each time you want to check.

A Telegram nudge goes out automatically on the 15th of every month to owners with Telegram configured, reminding them to run this before GSTR-3B filing.

History Pages

  1. Set From and To dates, optionally add a Status filter, tap Apply.
  2. Tap Detail on any row for the full line-item breakdown.
  3. Export CSV downloads the filtered list; Print gives a clean printout.
  4. On Dispatch and Issue History, Cancel reverses stock — this cannot be undone.
!
GRN History has no cancel or edit function. A GRN, once submitted, is permanent — use Settings → Stock Adjustment for genuine corrections only.
PART 5

Settings

Owner-only master configuration — company details, materials, staff, and integrations. Anyone can read this section for reference, but only the Owner role can actually open Settings; every other role is redirected to Dashboard automatically.

1. Company Details

Company Name, Address, Mobile, GSTIN, Bank Name, Account No, IFSC Code — appears on every challan and invoice. Do this on Day 1.

2. Raw Materials & Suppliers

Raw Materials: Name, Unit, Min Stock Level → Add Material. Bulk import via a downloadable template. Lite plan caps out at 250 active materials. To remove one, Deactivate — it keeps history, unlike Delete.

Suppliers: Name, Mobile, Address, GSTIN → Add. There's no Edit button — to correct a supplier, Deactivate the wrong one and add a new entry.

3. Clients, Products & BOM, Prices

Clients: Client Name (required), Email, Address, GSTIN → Add Client. Email is mandatory for the Send Challan and Send Invoice Copilot commands — a yellow "No email" badge flags anyone missing one.

Products & BOM: add products (with HSN/SAC) on the Products page — it's in the main menu, not inside Settings. In Bill of Materials, pick a product, search a raw material, enter Qty per ONE unit → Add to Recipe. A yellow "No BOM" badge means no raw material will be deducted.

Prices: two sections — Material Purchase Price and Product Selling Price. Every save adds a new price-history row; the system always uses the most recent price by date.

Challan & GRN Settings: starting numbers for challans, GRNs, and invoices; Unified (one series) or Split (separate per type) mode.

4. Staff Management & Telegram Alerts

Staff (Pro/Founder only): enter email + choose role → Send Invite. To change someone's role, Remove and re-invite — there's no direct role-change button.

  1. On Telegram: search @BotFather → /newbot → follow instructions → get a token.
  2. Search @userinfobot → send any message → get your Chat ID.
  3. Paste the Chat ID into Nexflow Settings → Save Telegram Settings.

After setup: a daily 8 AM summary, plus an instant alert after every Issue or Dispatch.

5. Agent Settings, CA Email & Stock Adjustment

Settings → Agent shows the Copilot's daily usage and limit tier (Standard: 30/day, Power: 100/day, Unlimited). Set the CA Export Email here — it's what powers the "CA la export pathav" command.

Stock Adjustment: use only for genuine corrections (physical count difference, damaged or lost material). Select Material → +/− → Quantity → enter a Reason (required) → Submit Adjustment.

PART 7

Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to fix them — fast.

❓ Can't Log In

  • Double-check email and password — tap the eye icon to reveal and check for typos.
  • No "Forgot Password" link — contact your Owner or Nexflow support.
  • New team members must be invited by the Owner from Settings → Staff.

❓ Scanner Not Working

  • Only works on Pro or Founder plan — Lite plan shows a lock message.
  • Only works in Chrome on an Android phone — not iPhone, not Safari, not desktop.
  • Camera denied: go to phone browser settings → re-allow camera → reload the page.
  • QR won't scan: check it's not damaged, hold steady inside the orange guide box, good lighting.

❓ Stock Showing Wrong Number

  • Tap the icon next to the material on Dashboard — see every GRN, Consumption, Adjustment.
  • Common cause: a GRN or Issue entered with the wrong quantity or wrong unit.
  • GRN entries cannot be edited — use Settings → Stock Adjustment for corrections.
  • Opening Stock must be entered as an Adjustment with the note text "Opening Stock" exactly.

❓ Challan or Invoice Email Not Received

  • Client must have an email saved under Settings → Clients — the most common cause.
  • Challan must be Confirmed, not Draft — drafts cannot be emailed.
  • Ask the client to check their Spam / Junk folder.
  • Ask the AI Copilot again — it will tell you exactly what went wrong.

❓ Invoice Not Generating

  • Plan must be Pro or Founder — not available on Lite.
  • Must be logged in as Owner or Supervisor — Accountant cannot generate.
  • The underlying dispatch must be Confirmed, not Draft or Cancelled.
  • Consolidated: if one already exists for that client + date range, the system reopens it — check the Invoices list first.

❓ GSTR-2B Reconciliation Problems

  • Plan gate: "GSTR-2B Reconciliation is available on the Pro and Founder plan. You're currently on Lite. Upgrade to get automatic ITC matching and never miss a blocked credit."
  • Role gate: "Only the owner or accountant can access this page." — Supervisor, Storekeeper, and Operator cannot open it even on Pro/Founder.
  • Invalid JSON: "This doesn't look like a valid GSTR-2B file. Download it from the GST portal under Returns → View GSTR-2B → Download JSON."
  • GSTIN mismatch shows a non-blocking warning naming both GSTINs — reconciliation still runs.
  • Missing supplier GSTINs prevent matching entirely — matching keys on supplier GSTIN + invoice number, so a supplier with no GSTIN on file (Settings → Suppliers) can never match.
  • Results disappeared after refresh — that's expected, they're session-only and never saved. Download the XLSX report if you need to keep a copy.

❓ AI Copilot Not Responding

  • Only visible to Owner and Supervisor — hidden for other roles by design.
  • First message of the day fails sometimes — send it again, it's a known one-time delay.
  • Daily limit reached — resets at midnight IST. Contact support to upgrade your tier.
  • Check your internet connection — the Copilot needs a server round-trip for every message.
